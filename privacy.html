Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Data We Collect

CareerSadhana is a static website. We collect only what you provide: name, email for account registration (stored in your browser's localStorage). No data is sent to any server.

2. Resume Privacy

Resume analysis is performed entirely in your browser. No resume content is uploaded to any server or stored anywhere. Files are processed locally and immediately discarded.

3. Camera & Microphone

Camera and microphone streams during the Versant test are used locally for monitoring simulation only. No video or audio is recorded, transmitted, or stored.

4. Contact

For privacy concerns, contact us via the About page contact form.
